Our story

Started with one windowsill.

Verdant began in 2020 when two friends filled a small apartment with plants and discovered how much better everyday life felt with a little more green around it.

Today, we work with independent growers to bring healthy, characterful plants to homes across Pakistan. Every plant is selected, packed, and cared for by people who love what they do.
